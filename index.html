Acceptable Use & Abuse Policy

Hosts on this network may not be used for spam, attacks, scanning without permission, or any other illegal or abusive activity.

Send abuse reports to childers@redbaron.net. Please include source IP addresses, timestamps with time zone, and relevant logs. Reports are acknowledged within 24 hours.

Confirmed abuse is stopped promptly, and the responsible host may be disconnected.
